# LGPD

## Privacy by design baseline

LogiPeople starts with minimization, purpose limitation, access control and audit primitives. Consent must not be used as a generic legal basis for all labor and HR processing.

## Initial privacy records

The initial model includes primitives for:

- Data classification.
- Sensitive data access logging.
- Audit logging.
- Field-level access decisions.
- Retention policy documentation placeholder.

## Restricted data examples

- Salary and compensation details.
- Bank data.
- Personal documents.
- Dependents.
- Medical information.
- Biometric data.
- Confidential performance records.
- Disciplinary measures.

## External integrations

LogiFlow and LogiDesk must receive only the minimum operational data required. Medical documents, salary, bank data, payslips, full personal documents and confidential evaluations must not be sent to those systems.

## Pending validation

LGPD processes, legal bases, retention, deletion assessments and incident response require DPO/privacy specialist validation before production use.
